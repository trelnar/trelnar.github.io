eBay's first generative AI feature

Most listings had no description. Between two similar items, buyers almost always chose the one that did. My team designed and shipped the Magical Listing Tool with eBay's AI team, concept to launch, September 2023. It drafts title, description, and item specifics. The first of a set of tools that take the effort out of listing: a photo, or a video with voice-over, and the rest follows.

The future of listing

Listing was the hardest thing we asked sellers to do, and a decade of fixes had left a dozen flows. The strategy we set in 2023 reduced it to two questions, what am I selling and how am I selling it, and the first one is the whole game. Answer it from whatever the seller has, a photo, a title, a number on a box, and fill in the rest.

Three rules carried the design: always listen, simply ask, be invisible. We proved the matching in a test harness before anyone built a screen, and ran discovery alongside delivery so the long-term work stopped losing to the next release.

The mobile seller sprint

Small businesses, 6 percent of sellers and 42 percent of revenue, lived on phones, where eBay trailed. I ran one of three Lighthouse sprints with Product, Marketing, and Engineering: three personas, journeys overlaid to find shared territory, concepts that graduate a seller from novice to pro. The vision was one flexible system, because a business grows. The concepts stay internal. The method is the point.

I got design into Product's six-month planning cycle, shaping roadmaps before they were set.
